## Privacy {#privacy}

This static demo does not collect data. Theme toggle uses `localStorage` only in the browser.

## Colophon {#colophon}

Fonts: **Bangers** and **Comic Neue** (loaded from Google Fonts in this demo, with files bundled under the theme `static/fonts/` for offline use). Halftone and shadows are pure CSS.
